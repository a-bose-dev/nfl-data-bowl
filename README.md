Separation leaders measures how open NFL receivers are at the moment the ball is thrown. From 2021 Big Data Bowl tracking, it records the yards between each wide or slot route runner and the nearest defender on the `pass_forward` frame, then summarizes those distances by coverage (man, zone, or red zone), alignment, and offensive personnel (running backs and tight ends). The browser ranks players by yards above the league average for the same configuration, with a snap minimum and the league distribution behind each mean. Coaches, scouts, and analysts can use it to see who consistently wins space in a given look, rather than who simply posts a high raw separation number.
